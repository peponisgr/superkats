SuperKats Studio

SuperKats Studio is a small personal tool made by the owner of The SuperKats: three real cats, Oliver, Last and Merlin, with superpowers added by AI.

The owner uses it on his own computer to publish his own short videos to his own Facebook Page, Instagram, YouTube channel and TikTok account in one step. On TikTok it only sends the video to the owner's drafts, and he publishes it himself in the TikTok app.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
