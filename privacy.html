Privacy Policy

SuperKats Studio

SuperKats Studio is a private publishing tool. It posts the owner's own videos to the owner's own social accounts and collects no data about anyone else.

Who uses it

One person: the owner of The SuperKats pages. The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data the app accesses

PlatformPermissionsUsed for
Facebookpages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_postsFind the owner's Page and publish Reels to it
Instagraminstagram_basic, instagram_content_publishFind the linked professional account and publish Reels to it
YouTubeyoutube.upload, youtube.readonlyUpload Shorts and show the channel name
TikTokuser.info.basic, video.uploadShow the account name and send videos to the owner's drafts

The app does not read followers, comments, messages, analytics or any other user's data.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens, account names, and the videos and captions the owner uploads are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to the platform a video is being published to.

Retention and deletion

Videos are deleted from the computer 30 days after they are published. Tokens are kept until the owner presses Disconnect in the app, which deletes them. Access can also be revoked from each platform: Facebook Settings → Business integrations; Google Account → Third-party apps with account access; TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Data deletion requests

Send a message to The SuperKats Page on Facebook. Any stored data is deleted within 7 days.
